## GitHub awesome-copilot

The `project-planner` skill adapts the MIT-licensed [breakdown-plan skill](https://github.com/github/awesome-copilot/tree/main/skills/breakdown-plan) from [github/awesome-copilot](https://github.com/github/awesome-copilot). Its [original MIT notice](skills/project-planner/LICENSE) is retained.
